# Community App - User Guide

Welcome to the **Community App**! This guide is designed to help community members navigate the platform, connect with their families, stay updated on community news, and take full advantage of our interactive features.

---

## Getting Started

### How to Register
1. Open the app or website and click on **Register**.
2. **Enter your Mobile Number**: Ensure this is the number you currently use.
3. **OTP Verification**: You will receive a 6-digit One Time Password (OTP). Enter this code to verify your phone number.
4. **Community Selection**: Select **Umarala Gam Samast Leuva Patel Samaj** (or the relevant community you belong to) from the dropdown list.
5. **Finding Your Family**: After successful registration, you can use the **Find Family** feature to see if your family is already registered and send a request to join them, or you can register as a new family head.

### How to Login
1. Click on **Login** from the main page.
2. Enter your registered **Mobile Number**.
3. **OTP Verification**: Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your phone. (For testing purposes, the default OTP is `221221`).
4. **Troubleshooting**: If the OTP doesn't arrive, click "Resend OTP" after 30 seconds. Make sure your network connection is stable.

## Dashboard
Your dashboard is the central hub of your community app experience.
- **Tiles & Navigation**: You will see various tiles (e.g., Members, Notice Board, Gallery). Clicking on any tile takes you directly to that section.
- **Notification Bell**: Located at the top right, this bell shows a red badge if you have unread notifications (e.g., family requests, new notices, or approvals).

## Family Tree

The Family Tree is a visual representation of your relationships within the community.

### Viewing Your Family Tree
- **Parents & Spouses**: Placed directly above or adjacent to your profile card.
- **Children**: Branching below you and your spouse.
- **Siblings**: Displayed parallel to you under your parents.
- **Extended Family**: The tree seamlessly maps out uncles, aunts, nephews, nieces, and cousins based on interconnected profiles.
- **In-Laws Section**: Shows the family tree from the perspective of your spouse's side.

### Adding a Relationship
1. Navigate to the **Family Tree** tab.
2. Click the **+ Add Relationship** button near the person you want to connect a member to.
3. **Search Member**: Type the name or phone number of the community member.
4. **Select Relationship Type**: Choose how they are related to the selected person (e.g., Father, Wife, Son).
5. **Confirm**: Click submit. 
   - *Note*: The system will automatically compute the inverse relationship (e.g., if you add them as "Father", you become "Son" or "Daughter"). The request may require admin approval or family head approval.

### Understanding Relationship Labels
- **Direct**: Father, Mother, Son, Daughter, Husband, Wife, Brother, Sister.
- **Extended**: Uncle, Aunt, Nephew, Niece, Cousin.
- **In-Laws**: Father-in-Law, Mother-in-Law, Brother-in-Law, Sister-in-Law, Son-in-Law, Daughter-in-Law.

## Finding Your Family (New Users)

If your family members are already in the app, you don't need to create a new family from scratch.

- **What is "Find Family"?**: A smart search tool to locate your existing family tree.
- **How Smart Search Works**: It uses your surname, village, and other details to suggest potential family matches.
- **Sending a Join Request**: Once you find your family, click "Request to Join".
- **Tracking Your Request**: You can track the status under **My Requests**.
- **When Approved**: Once the Family Head or Admin approves, you are instantly merged into their family tree.

## Members Directory
The Members Directory is a complete list of registered community members.
- **Browsing**: Scroll through the list to view community members.
- **Search**: Use the search bar to find people by Name or Phone Number.
- **Filters**:
  - **All**: Shows everyone.
  - **Family Heads**: Shows only designated family representatives.
  - **Committee**: Shows only governing committee members.
  - **My Family**: Quickly filters to show only members of your immediate/extended family.
- **Viewing Details**: Click on a member's card to view their full profile, contact info, and family ties.

---

## Notice Board
Stay updated with official community announcements.
- **Types of Notices**:
  - 📰 **News**: General community updates.
  - 📅 **Event**: Upcoming celebrations or gatherings (includes Date & Location).
  - 🤝 **Meeting**: Committee or general meetings.
  - 🕊️ **Death Notice**: Condolences and obituary announcements.
- **Filtering & Reading**: Use the top filters to sort by type, and click any notice to read the full description and view attached photos.

---

## Committee Members
View the leaders who govern the community.
- **Who they are**: Elected or appointed community leaders.
- **Role Hierarchy**: Sorted from highest position (President, Vice President) down to general Committee Members.
- **Contacting**: Click on their profile to find their email or phone number for official queries.

---

## Business Directory
Support community-owned businesses!
- **Finding Businesses**: Browse all registered businesses.
- **Category Filters**: Easily filter by sectors like Real Estate, IT, Retail, Healthcare, etc.
- **Viewing Details**: Click a business to see what they offer.
- **Quick Actions**: Use the direct Call, WhatsApp, and Google Maps buttons.
- **Adding Your Business**: Click "Add Business", fill in your details, category, and upload a cover photo to list your own venture.

---

## My Profile
Manage your personal identity on the app.
- **Viewing & Editing**: Go to "Profile" from the sidebar/bottom nav. Click Edit to update your email, address, or bio.
- **Uploading Photo**: Click the camera icon on your avatar to upload a clear profile picture.
- **Role Badges**: Badges like "Admin", "Committee", or "Family Head" will display under your name if applicable.

---

## Donors
Acknowledge the generous contributors to our community.
- **Lifetime vs Recent**: The page is divided into "Lifetime Donors" (indicated by gold badges) and "Recent Donors" (yearly or one-time contributors).
- **Browsing**: You can filter donors by donation year or category (e.g., Education Fund, Community Development).

---

## Abroad Members
Connect with community members living overseas.
- **Finding Members**: View the directory of members living internationally.
- **Country Filters**: Easily filter to find people living in the USA, UK, Australia, etc.

---

## Marksheets
A space to celebrate educational achievements.
- **Uploading**: Students or parents can upload marksheets (10th, 12th, Degree) via the "Upload" button. Include percentage and standard.
- **Approval Status**: Uploads start as "Pending". Once reviewed by an Admin, they will show as "Approved" or "Rejected".
- **Ranks**: Approved marksheets that qualify for awards will display a Rank badge (e.g., Rank 1, Rank 2).

---

## Photo Gallery
Relive community moments.
- **Browsing Albums**: Albums are categorized by event name and year.
- **Viewing Photos**: Click an album folder to view all high-quality photos from that event. 

---

## Notifications
Stay informed of important actions.
- **Meaning**: You get notified when someone requests to join your family, when an AI match is found, or when a notice is posted.
- **Marking as Read**: Click a notification to mark it as read and navigate to the relevant page.
- **Types**: Approvals, Rejections, System Alerts, and Messages.
